Enrollment Policy

Clear expectations before payment.

Draft for review before paid enrollment opens.

Pre-registration

Pre-registration does not guarantee a place, creates no payment obligation and is not final enrollment. All offerings remain subject to final MTO approval and student eligibility.

Tuition

Founding tuition is $900 plus applicable taxes for the first 30 eligible students accepted after approval. Regular tuition is $1,200 plus applicable taxes.

Planned program

20 hours of online classroom instruction, 10 hours of flexible online instruction and 10 hours of private in-car training, subject to final MTO approval.

Scheduling

A preferred training week is a request, not a confirmed booking. Dates depend on eligibility, availability, weather, safety and the student’s training plan.

Cancellations and refunds

Notice periods, rescheduling charges, missed-lesson terms and refund rules will be presented in writing before payment is collected.

Payment

Summit accepts secure electronic payments by Visa Debit and major credit cards. Cash, e-transfers and cheques are not accepted. Payments are documented and receipted.
