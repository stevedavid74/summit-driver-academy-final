Official Brand Guide

Summit Driver Academy

Driving Confidence to the Highest Standard.

Brand Position

Summit is a premium driver education company built around confidence, accountability, digital visibility and consistent instruction. The brand should feel refined, calm, trustworthy and modern—never loud, cheap or gimmicky.

Official Logo

Use the logo on dark, uncluttered backgrounds with generous clear space. Do not stretch, recolour, crop, tilt or place over busy photography.

Typography

Cormorant Garamond is used for premium headlines and emotional statements. Inter is used for navigation, body copy, forms, labels and digital interfaces.

Signature Brand Systems

The Summit Path™

The complete student journey from online preparation through final coaching.

The Summit Standard™

The consistent quality and accountability applied to every lesson and instructor.

Summit Score™

The measurable student skill and readiness framework.

The Summit Difference

The combined value of instruction, technology, communication and standards.

Voice and Messaging

  • Speak clearly, calmly and confidently.
  • Focus on lifelong safe driving—not only passing a road test.
  • Use premium language without exaggeration or unsupported promises.
  • Address parents’ need for trust and students’ need for confidence.
  • Avoid slang, pressure tactics and bargain-focused language.

Photography Direction

Use clean, high-contrast automotive photography with natural confidence, professional instructors, real learning moments and uncluttered environments. Avoid generic stock imagery that feels staged, childish or inexpensive.

Launch Rule

Any regulatory approval, success-rate claim, pricing, tax wording, age eligibility or course-credit statement must be confirmed and kept current before publication.
