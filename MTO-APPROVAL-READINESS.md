# Summit Driver Academy — MTO Approval Readiness

Status date: August 27, 2026  
Purpose: internal working checklist for a future Beginner Driver Education (BDE) application. This is not proof of approval and does not replace instructions supplied directly by the Ministry of Transportation.

## Approval gate

- [ ] Email `driver.education@ontario.ca` to start the application process.
- [ ] Obtain the current BDE policy manual, registration package, instructor forms, BDE-system registration link, and audit checklist from MTO.
- [ ] Do not represent Summit as government approved until MTO confirms approval.
- [x] Keep public registration as no-payment pre-registration.
- [x] Keep Stripe live payments locked; sandbox testing only.

## Business documentation

- [x] Ontario corporation registered as Summit Driver Academy Inc.
- [ ] Assemble the current corporate profile, registered address, and signing-authority documents.
- [ ] Confirm municipal zoning and any required local business permissions for the operating location.
- [ ] Obtain commercial general liability coverage required by the application package.
- [ ] Obtain vehicle insurance with the required driving-school endorsement and at least the coverage specified by the current regulation.
- [ ] Confirm the tax treatment of tuition and GST/HST registration with an accountant or CRA before enabling Stripe Tax.

## Curriculum decision — required before course production

Choose one route and retain the supporting agreement:

- [ ] **In-house curriculum:** develop Summit's full curriculum, submit it to MTO, receive approval, and retain the Declaration of BDE ministry-approved curriculum agreement.
- [ ] **Third-party curriculum:** purchase/licence an MTO-approved curriculum and retain the curriculum purchase agreement.

Do not publish a course as MTO-approved or accept payment for an approved BDE program until the selected curriculum and Summit's school application are approved.

## Required instructional structure

- [ ] 20 hours of classroom, digital, or blended instruction.
- [ ] 10 hours of flexible instruction.
- [ ] 10 hours of in-vehicle instruction.
- [ ] Minimum 40 instructional hours in total, excluding breaks and travel time.
- [ ] Entire course must be completed within one year.
- [ ] Maximum five hours of classroom instruction per student per day.
- [ ] Maximum two hours of in-vehicle instruction per student per day.
- [ ] When a student receives five classroom hours in one day, limit in-vehicle instruction that day to one hour.
- [ ] For in-person classroom instruction, cap classes at 40 students.
- [ ] For virtual classroom instruction, cap classes at 20 students.
- [ ] Ensure indirect, interactive, and experiential methods comprise at least 50% of classroom hours.
- [ ] Ensure direct instruction comprises no more than 40% of classroom hours.
- [ ] Ensure independent learning comprises no more than 20% of classroom hours.

Summit's operational rule of three students trained by an instructor in one day may remain in place, provided every individual student's daily limits and the maximum number of students simultaneously in a training vehicle are respected.
