# Summit 30-Hour Theory Program — Build Brief

This brief begins course production only after Summit chooses an in-house or licensed third-party curriculum route. It deliberately does not claim MTO approval.

## Proposed delivery model

- 20 hours: supervised virtual/digital classroom instruction.
- 10 hours: flexible instruction using approved digital activities, Homelinks, classroom activities, simulator instruction, or additional in-vehicle instruction.
- 10 hours: private in-vehicle instruction tracked separately by the existing portal.

The final delivery mix must match the curriculum approved by MTO.

## Course system requirements

- Account identity verification and secure individual access.
- Module prerequisites and ordered progression.
- Active instructional time tracking; breaks and idle time do not count.
- Log-on, log-off, pause, resume, and completion-event history.
- Daily-hour limits enforced before additional content can begin.
- Knowledge checks, scored assessments, remediation, and reassessment history.
- Instructor supervision/resource contact for digital learning.
- Accessibility support, captions/transcripts, keyboard operation, and readable mobile layouts.
- Tamper-resistant attendance, assessment, correction, and audit records.
- Owner/manager compliance view and downloadable MTO evidence.
- Parent progress view limited to the connected student.
- No course-completion certification until every approved component is complete.

## Content-production template for every module

1. MTO curriculum-standard references.
2. Learning objectives.
3. Approved instructional minutes.
4. Delivery type and teaching-method classification.
5. Instructor preparation and required materials.
6. Student lesson content.
7. Interactive or experiential activity.
8. Knowledge check and answer key.
9. Remediation activity.
10. Accessibility accommodations.
11. Completion evidence retained by the system.

## Decision needed before the next build phase

Record whether Summit will:

- develop and submit an in-house curriculum; or
- purchase/licence a currently MTO-approved digital curriculum.

That decision determines whether the next phase is full instructional-content production or integration with an approved third-party course platform.
